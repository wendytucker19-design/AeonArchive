## Exhibition artwork

The hero image (`/exhibition/museum-hero.webp`) and the three selected exhibition images (`cobalt-vessel.webp`, `meridian-keeper.webp`, and `nocturne-dial.webp`) are AI-generated artistic visualisations created for this redesign. They are inspired by the approved museum concept. They illustrate the exhibition and are not documentary photographs of the reference objects. The original, attributed photographs remain available in each object's Source photograph view.

## Interactive studies and geographic data

The six interactive object models are procedural artistic studies built in Three.js. Their shapes are interpretive. They are not scans of the reference objects.

The atlas uses the existing local Natural Earth 1:110m land dataset (`/maps/land.geojson`). Natural Earth geographic data is public domain. See https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.

## Typography

Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans are served locally. Their SIL Open Font License files are included in `/fonts`.
